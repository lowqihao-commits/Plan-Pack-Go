# Plan Pack Go — Product Brief

## Product definition

- **App name:** Plan Pack Go
- **Project type:** cross-platform travel planner concept
- **Current phase:** UX/UI definition and interactive prototype
- **Platform direction:** mobile-first; initially implemented as a mobile-sized web UI for fast iteration
- **Working slogan:** `Plan smart. Pack right. Go ready.` — **LOCKED / APPROVED for the current prototype**

Plan Pack Go is a travel planner with itinerary, route, trip-cost and group-coordination functions. It is not only a packing app.

## Core problem

Most travel planners stop after arranging an itinerary. Travellers must still manually translate days, activities, weather and group responsibilities into what they should pack. This leads to:

- overpacking and underpacking;
- forgotten activity-specific items;
- clothing quantities that do not match the trip;
- duplicate or missing shared items; and
- unclear responsibility during group travel.

## Core value proposition

> A Travel Planner that converts your actual itinerary into an actionable, coordinated packing plan.

## Product philosophy

> AI recommends, user decides.

The system may analyse, explain, rank and propose. It must not silently finalize places, change itineraries, insert packing items, remove packed decisions, or reassign shared responsibilities.

## Required baseline functions — LOCKED / APPROVED

- itinerary building;
- route planning and optimization;
- lightweight trip-cost tracking;
- group preference synchronization;
- Solo and Group travel support; and
- lightweight plan-change handling.

## Core differentiator — LOCKED / APPROVED

**Smart Packing & Luggage Coordination** connects recommendations to the actual itinerary, activities, duration, weather and Solo/Group context. It produces explainable `Needed` and `Possibly Useful` suggestions, then supports personal checklists and Group shared-item responsibility.

## Primary users

- solo travellers;
- groups of friends or family travelling together.

## Why mobile-first

The product is used while packing and during the trip. A mobile format better supports in-trip navigation, quick itinerary changes, group coordination, notifications and checklist use near luggage. The prototype remains cross-platform in concept and uses a web implementation only to accelerate testing.

## Prototype success definition

The prototype succeeds when a reviewer can understand and interact through both branches, see how final places become a day-based itinerary, and see how that itinerary becomes a personal or coordinated packing plan. Visual polish must support this story without making the product feel like a complex AI control centre.
